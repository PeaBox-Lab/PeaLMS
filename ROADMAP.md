# Product Roadmap

## v0.1.x — MVP stabilization

- Harden import validation and mapping profiles.
- Add manual assignment interface and saved report filters.
- Add API tests, browser tests and accessibility regression tests.
- Add database migration tooling and configurable seed behavior.

## v0.2.0 — Identity and enterprise controls

- OIDC/SAML federation and server-side RBAC scopes.
- PostgreSQL persistence, background workers and scheduled jobs.
- Organization hierarchy and manager subtree authorization.
- Recurring certification, exemptions, approvals and escalation reminders.
- Notification templates and email delivery.

## v0.3.0 — Learning delivery

- Versioned content publishing workflow.
- SCORM launch and progress adapter.
- Assessments, attempts, grading, surveys and certificates.
- Instructor-led sessions, attendance, waitlists and calendars.
- Observation checklists and evidence attachments.

## v0.4.0 — Scale and ecosystem

- Multi-tenant or branch experiences and delegated administration.
- REST service accounts, signed webhooks and event streaming.
- Search service, content repositories and virtual classroom connectors.
- xAPI/LRS and LTI 1.3 integration.
- Advanced report builder and BI datasets.

## v0.5.0 — Skills and experience

- Skills taxonomy, role profiles and learning-to-skill mapping.
- Personalized paths, collections, recommendations and communities.
- Progressive and native mobile improvements, including offline use cases.
- Digital badges and portable credentials.

## v1.0.0 — Enterprise production release

- High availability, disaster recovery and formal service objectives.
- WCAG 2.2 AA conformance evidence.
- Security and privacy certification readiness.
- Global localization and data-residency options.
- Audited upgrade, backup, restore and support processes.

## Future — Governed AI

- Human-reviewed course and assessment drafting.
- Grounded conversational discovery and tutoring.
- Administrator assistance for safe drafts and exception triage.
- Skills inference and risk analytics only after data-governance gates.
